Digital notifications trigger dopamine pathways that encourage compulsive checking behavior.
Auditory and visual notification banners increase task resumption lag by over 400%.
Implementing notification batching—checking emails and chat messages only at designated intervals (e.g. 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM)—preserves up to 90 minutes of productive cognitive capacity per workday.
Shielding work hours from non-essential digital stimulation is the most effective single intervention for elevating deep work focus scores.
